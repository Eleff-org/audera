A new era of composable audio systems

We built Audera to listen to music in any room, from any source, on DIY-friendly components and speakers - open-source plans coming soon.

Audera leverages open standards (Snapcast, CamillaDSP) to provide DSP-corrected, multi-room, synchronized audio playback.

Unlike proprietary systems like Sonos, WiiM, Roon, Bluesound, or Bose, which require cloud accounts or proprietary hardware, Audera is fully private, open-source, and runs on your own Raspberry Pi hardware.
