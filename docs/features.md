# Features

The **Audera Streamer** hosts a local web app for managing devices (incl. latency adjustment), sources, player streams, and per-player DSP pipelines.

<table>
<tr>
<td align="center" width="50%" valign="top">

<img src="../website/assets/players.webp" alt="Players screen showing grouped rooms in one synchronized stream with per-player volume" width="260">

**Players** - _Listen to **Audera** everywhere_

Setup players wherever you listen and balance player volume for seemless listening throughout every room.

</td>
<td align="center" width="50%" valign="top">

<img src="../website/assets/sources.webp" alt="Sources screen listing AirPlay 2, Spotify Connect, and PlexAmp inputs" width="260">

**Sources** - _Cast to **Audera** from the sources you already use_

`AirPlay 2` for iPhone, iPad or Mac, `Spotify Connect` for Spotify Premium subscribers, and `PlexAmp` for Plex library casting.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td align="center" width="50%" valign="top">

<img src="../website/assets/player-settings.webp" alt="Player settings screen with rename, latency offset, volume reset, and device details" width="260">

**Player settings** - _Tune every room._

Rename a player and manage per-player timing to compensate for room accoustics and DAC latency.

</td>
<td align="center" width="50%" valign="top">

<img src="../website/assets/dsp.webp" alt="DSP screen with a parametric EQ, biquad bands, live response curve, and pre-amp" width="260">

**DSP** - _Per-room correction_

Configure a parametric EQ per player via presets, imported configs, or crafted biquad bands.

</td>
